GOVERNMENT OF KERALA

A municipality is an urban local self-government institution responsible for the administration and development of urban areas. It plays an important role in providing essential services such as water supply, sanitation, waste management, road maintenance, public health, and urban planning. The selected municipality serves as the study area for this WebGIS project. The study focuses on understanding the geographical characteristics, spatial distribution of important features, land use patterns, road networks, population distribution, and public facilities within the municipal area. WebGIS (Web-based Geographic Information System) is a technology that enables geographical information to be stored, analysed, visualised, and shared through the internet. In this project, spatial layers and administrative data are integrated into an interactive web interface to support spatial analysis and decision-making.
